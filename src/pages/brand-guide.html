Voice

Active, direct, no hype. Full detail in writing-guide.md.

Do

  • Lead with the payload. Concrete numbers over adjectives.
  • Active voice. Short sentence. Then a longer one. Then short again.
  • Specific CTAs — “Start a Project”, “Hire a Tech Lead”.
  • Say what needed a human, and what’s still left. Honesty is the differentiator.

Don’t

  • No corporate filler or passive voice.
  • No “Learn More” / “Get Started” CTAs.
  • Avoid these words:
utilizeleverageseamlessrobustempowercutting-edgesynergysolution
