About

Small studio, unreasonable range

LumiVerse is a founder-led AI product studio in Zagreb. We do two things - build our own products, and embed with teams to build theirs - and we're deliberately generalist about it. The same hands that rebuilt a ten-year-old rent-a-car system on a modern stack also run an agentic simulation station that computes plasma-display physics. Range isn't a lack of focus. It's the focus.

We eat our own cooking

Titlomat, Tvrtko.ai and Country Guides are LumiVerse products in production, with real users - not slideware. We know what shipping costs because we pay it ourselves.

We embed, we don't hover

For a UK intelligence platform we've owned architecture, a 17-service system and release sign-off for over a year. We join your team and ship with it - not from a deck.

Honest about the hard parts

Every case study here says what needed a human and what's still rough. Generation is cheap; judgement isn't. The moat is the verifier, not the generator.
