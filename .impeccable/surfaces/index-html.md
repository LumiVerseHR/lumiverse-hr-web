# Homepage (v3)

Scope: the lumiverse.hr homepage, EN and HR, rebuilt in Next.js. Mode: Persuade.
Audience: high-end clients choosing an agency for high-impact AI work. Action: Start a Project (the brief form). Proof: the flagships Moj Kolega, Bridj and Air-laser with real screenshots, then the compact index of the other case studies. SME packages, tech-lead hiring and technical-founder offers live on their own landing pages and are only linked from here.
Approved comp: .impeccable/mocks/comp-3-screens-in-model.png (hero). Section reference: .impeccable/mocks/comp-2-maquette-case-grid.png (framed case grid right after the hero).

## Direction contract

THESIS: We build Better Humans, literally: every shipped project is a white scale model on one plinth, and tiny coral figures are the people it amplifies. It refuses the AI-agency default of a dark field with neon glow and a bento grid, and the showreel-over-video agency standard.
OWN-WORLD: A dark gallery (#0E0F12 walls, #1C1D21 floor) under one warm raking spotlight. Models in white card #EDEBE7 and basswood #C9A57A. Coral #FF4D4D only on the human figures, the logo dot and the primary CTA. Real product screenshots appear as lit screens mounted on the models and as framed gallery placards. Small black placards carry project names in a condensed caption face. Heavy white grotesk headlines.
STORY: In one viewport the visitor sees a studio that builds real, varied things, each proven by a real screenshot. Scrolling enters the model: each flagship opens into its framed case placard with real numbers, then the index of other work, the thesis (generation is cheap, judgement isn't), services, and the close. The visitor believes the studio is ambitious and trustworthy, and starts a project.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar: wordmark left, nav (Work, Services, Research, Studio, Contact) centred, EN/HR and a small coral Start a Project button right. Left third: "We build / Better / Humans." at monumental scale, the subline, a coral Start a Project button and an Explore the work link. Right two thirds: a real-time 3D plinth receding in perspective with three models (a storefront for Moj Kolega, a 17-slab tower for Bridj, a wire sphere above a screen for Air-laser), each showing its real screenshot as a lit screen, black placards on the plinth, coral figures. Bottom left: a scroll hint; bottom right: drag to orbit.
FORM: Scale Model (an architect's maquette under gallery light), built as the user-pinned 3D + FX lane, combined with the premium-agency standard's concrete case grid at the user's request. Seed key 4c8b5bef.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
