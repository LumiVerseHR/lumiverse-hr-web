One grid, one stroke, one angle

Every measurement is a multiple of the 16-unit gap. The hook follows a rule: extend the right arm's inner edge and it meets the left arm exactly where the hook sits, so the hook is the right arm passing under the left one.

    03

    The wordmark is real type now

    The W2 file traced a bitmap into 3,349 tiny curves, and the edges wobble. The new wordmark is outlined from Golos Text 500 with the font's own kerning and the header's −0.02em tracking. It renders identically everywhere and weighs about 3 KB.
