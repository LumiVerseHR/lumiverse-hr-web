Before you use them

  1. The legal footer needs two more facts

    Croatian business letters must also name the management board and state whether the share capital is paid in full. Neither is in the brand data yet, so the letterhead and memo footers leave them out. Tell me both and the generator adds them everywhere.

    Open

  2. Word templates need Golos Text installed

    The DOCX files are set in Golos Text. On a machine without it, Word substitutes a default font. The font is in the zip's fonts/ folder and is free to install.

    Check in Word

  3. The email signature points at the live site

    Its logo loads from lumiverse.hr/assets/brand/kit/, so it shows only once this is published.

    After publishing

  4. Names, roles and phone numbers are placeholders

    Business card, letter sample and signature say "Ime Prezime" and "Funkcija". Send the team list for personalised versions.
